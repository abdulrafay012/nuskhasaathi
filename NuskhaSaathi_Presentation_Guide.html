1 · Masla · The problem

Nuskha mojood hai, lekin mareez usay parh nahi sakta.

The prescription exists. The patient can't read it.

Pakistan mein doctor jaldi mein nuskha likhta hai: English mein dawaiyon ke naam, Latin shorthand, aur aisi likhai jo sirf pharmacist ke liye hoti hai. Jo shakhs English nahi parh sakta, uske liye ye kaghaz bekaar hai. A Pakistani doctor writes a nuskha in a hurry, using English drug names, Latin shorthand and handwriting meant for the pharmacist. An adult who can't read English gets nothing useful from it.
Nuqsan roz ka hai: din mein teen ki jagah do baar dawai, bukhar utarte hi course chhor dena, aur do alag doctors ki aisi dawaiyan saath le lena jo kabhi saath nahi leni chahiye. The harm is everyday: twice a day instead of three times, a course stopped when the fever breaks, and two medicines from two different doctors that should never be taken together.
Hamari baat: masla doctor ya dawai tak pohanch ka nahi, samajh ka hai. Telemedicine doctor tak le jati hai, pharmacy app dawai ghar bhej deti hai. Dono wahan ruk jate hain jahan nuskha mareez ke haath mein aata hai. NuskhaSaathi wahi se shuru hota hai. The failure isn't access, it's understanding. Telemedicine and pharmacy apps both stop where the paper reaches the patient, and that's where NuskhaSaathi starts.

2 · Kaise kaam karta hai · How it works

Photo se awaaz tak chaar qadam. Inmein se ek jaan boojh kar AI nahi hai.

Four steps from photo to voice. One of them is deliberately not AI.

  1. Likhai parhnaRead the handwriting
    Qwen-VL Plus nuskhe ka text nikalta hai: Urdu, English aur shorthand sab. Jo lafz samajh na aaye, woh [unclear] likh deta hai, andaza nahi lagata. Qwen-VL Plus extracts the text. Words it can't read come back as [unclear] instead of being guessed.
    POST /upload · qwen-vl-plus
  2. Dawaiyon ki list bananaStructure the medicines
    Qwen Plus text ko dawaiyon ki list mein badalta hai: dose, kitni baar, kitne din. Ghalat spelling theek karta hai (Paracetmol → Paracetamol), aur har dawai par Confident ya Uncertain ka nishan lagata hai. Qwen Plus builds a list with dosage, frequency and duration, fixes misreads, and marks each medicine Confident or Uncertain.
    POST /identify-medicines · qwen-plus
  3. Safety check, seedhe code meinSafety check, in plain code
    15 tasdeeq shuda khatarnak jooron ki table, is nuskhe aur mareez ke purane nuskhon dono par check hoti hai. Brand naam bhi samajhti hai: Brufen + Disprin = Ibuprofen + Aspirin. Yahan AI nahi, is liye warning na ghalat banti hai na chhoot-ti hai. A fixed table of 15 dangerous pairs checks this prescription and the patient's others, including brand names. No model is involved, so a warning can't be invented or missed.
    checkInteractions() · deterministic
  4. Urdu mein bol kar samjhanaExplain it out loud in Urdu
    Qwen Plus har dawai ke liye chhoti, izzat wali Urdu wazahat likhta hai. Browser usay 0.85× raftaar par bolta hai taake aasani se samajh aaye. Qwen Plus writes a short Urdu explanation per medicine. The browser reads it aloud at 0.85× speed.
    POST /instructions · qwen-plus + Web Speech API

4 · Daira · Scope

Ye kya hal karta hai, aur kya nahi

What it solves, and what it doesn't.

Hal karta haiSolves

  • Nuskha parh na sakna. Urdu mein bol kar batata hai, parhna zaroori nahi.Can't read it: spoken Urdu, no reading needed.
  • Shorthand ki uljhan. BD, TDS, 5/7 saaf waqt aur dinon mein badal jate hain.Shorthand becomes plain times and days.
  • Mukhtalif doctors ki dawaiyon ka takrao. Saare nuskhe ek saath check hote hain.Dangerous combinations across doctors are checked together.
  • Bhool jana ke kab kya leni hai. Jaane pehchane waqton mein rozana schedule.A daily schedule in familiar times of day.
  • Ghar wale nazar na rakh sakein. Caregiver link, PDF aur doctor summary.Family can follow along via link, PDF and summary.
  • AI ki chhupi ghaltiyan. Uncertain nishan, mareez ki tasheeh, aur dose ya waqt ka kabhi andaza nahi.Uncertain badges, patient correction, and no guessed doses or timings.
  • Maloom allergies (naya). Mareez ki likhi allergy se naam mile to warning.Known allergies flagged by name (new).

Hal nahi kartaDoesn't solve

  • Tashkhees ya ilaaj chunna. Sirf doctor ka likha hua samjhata hai. Ye jaan boojh kar hai.Diagnosis or choosing treatment. By design.
  • Ghalat nuskha. Agar doctor se ghalti hui to app wahi ghalti samjha degi.A wrong prescription gets explained faithfully.
  • Mukammal dawai safety. 15 jore achi shuruaat hai, poora clinical database nahi.15 pairs is a careful start, not a full database.
  • Allergy ke khandaan. Penicillin allergy par amoxicillin ki warning nahi aayegi.Penicillin allergy won't flag amoxicillin.
  • Bohat gandi likhai par sabit accuracy. Abhi sirf 2 saaf tasveeron par test hua hai.Accuracy on messy handwriting isn't proven yet.
  • Smartphone ya internet na ho. Phone zaroori hai. PDF kuch had tak madad karti hai.Needs a smartphone and internet. The PDF partly helps.
  • Har phone par Urdu awaaz. Browser par munhasir hai. Urdu awaaz na ho to app notice dikhati hai.Urdu voice depends on the browser; the app shows a notice when missing.
  • Dawai ki qeemat. Sasti dawai wala feature prototype hai, qeematein tasdeeq shuda nahi.Cost: the alternatives feature is a prototype with unverified prices.

6 · Sawal jawab · Q&A prep

Judges jo sawal pooch sakte hain

Questions judges are likely to ask.

Asal doctor ki likhai par OCR kitna sahi hai?How accurate is the OCR on real doctor handwriting?
Seedha jawab dein: benchmark par 7 mein se 7 dawaiyan sahi, dose aur waqt bhi sahi, kuch apni taraf se nahi banaya. Lekin sirf 2 saaf tasveerein thin. Gandi likhai, kam roshni aur Urdu script agle 10 test hain. Phir safety net batayein: Uncertain nishan, [unclear], aur mareez ki tasheeh.7 of 7 correct with nothing invented, but on 2 clean images. Messy handwriting is next. Then mention the safety net.

"On our benchmark it got all 7 medicines right and invented nothing. But that's 2 clean images. Messy handwriting, bad lighting and Urdu script are the next cases we're adding."

Aap kehte hain tashkhees nahi karte, phir symptom triage kyun hai?You say it never diagnoses, but there's a symptom triage feature.
Ye project ka sab se kamzor nukta hai. Aaj raat faisla karein. Ya to triage demo mein na dikhayein, ya isay mehdood tareeqe se batayein: sirf ye check karta hai ke alamat pehle se likhi dawai ka maloom side effect hai ya nahi, aur jawab hamesha "doctor ko dikhayein" ya "emergency jayein" hota hai. Abhi ka prompt is se zyada khula hai, waqt ho to usay tang kar dein.Weakest point. Either skip it in the demo or describe it narrowly. The current prompt is broader; tighten it if you have time.

"It only checks whether a symptom is a known side effect of medicines already prescribed, and the answer is always 'see your doctor' or 'go to emergency'. It never suggests a treatment."

Sirf 15 jore kyun?Why only 15 interaction pairs?
Jaan boojh kar. Har jora achi tarah documented hai aur hum har ek ka difa kar sakte hain. Bari list jo tasdeeq na ho, zyada khatarnak hai. Barhane se pehle pharmacist se review karwana hai.On purpose: each pair is defensible. A large unverified list is more dangerous.

"On purpose. Every pair is well documented and we can defend each one. A large list we can't verify would be more dangerous. Before it grows, we want a pharmacist to review it."

Agar AI dawai ka naam ghalat parh le to?What if the AI misreads a medicine name?
Peele rang mein Uncertain dikhta hai. Mareez theek kar sakta hai, jis se safety check dobara chalta hai aur purani Urdu wazahat mit jati hai, taake ghalat baat na bole. Purani ghalat baat sunane se kuch na sunana behtar hai.It shows as Uncertain; correcting it re-runs the safety check and deletes the old explanation.
Paisa kaun dega? Business model kya hai?Who pays? What's the business model?
Mashwara, jis par aap khud yaqeen karein woh chunein: mareez ke liye muft. Clinic aur pharmacy chains paisa dein, kyunke dawai theek lene se mareez kam wapas aate hain. Doosra option insurance aur NGOs ke chronic bimari programs. Har nuskhe ki AI cost chand API calls hai, aur awaaz phone par muft chalti hai.Free for patients; clinics and pharmacy chains pay. Insurers and NGOs second. Cost is a few API calls per prescription.
Google Lens aur Google Translate se kya farq hai?How is this different from Google Lens plus Translate?
Lens sirf alfaaz parhta hai. Usay nahi pata ke BD ka matlab din mein do baar hai, woh do doctors ki dawaiyon ke takrao ki warning nahi deta, schedule nahi banata, ye nahi batata ke kab yaqeen nahi, aur jo parh nahi sakta us se baat nahi karta.Lens reads words. It doesn't decode BD, warn about interactions, build a schedule, flag uncertainty, or speak to someone who can't read.
Mareez ki privacy ka kya?What about patient privacy?
Password hash hote hain. Har nuskha sirf uske malik ko dikhta hai; kisi aur ka nuskha bilkul aisa lagta hai jaise mojood hi nahi. Caregiver link lambe random token hain jo band kiye ja sakte hain. Demo ka sara data farzi hai. Imaandari se kahein ke production mein encrypted storage aur data protection review chahiye hoga.Hashed passwords, owner-only access, revocable random links, fictional demo data. Production needs encrypted storage and a data-protection review.
Kya ye offline ya bina smartphone ke chalta hai?Does it work offline or without a smartphone?
Photo aur AI wale qadam ke liye internet chahiye. Awaaz phone par chalti hai aur history dobara suni ja sakti hai. Jin ghar walon ke paas smartphone nahi, unke liye print hui Urdu PDF. Saade phones tak pohanchne ke liye WhatsApp reminders plan mein hain.Photo and AI need internet. Read-aloud runs on device. The PDF covers people without phones; WhatsApp reminders are planned.
